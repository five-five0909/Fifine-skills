# Academic Writing

Use this skill for STEM academic writing and editing.

## Style

- Maintain a formal, objective, concise, and logically rigorous tone.
- Preserve the scientific meaning, data, assumptions, and conclusions unless the user explicitly asks for revision.
- Prefer clear structure: research background, problem, method, result, discussion, limitation.
- Avoid colloquial phrasing, unsupported claims, and invented references.

## Source Handling

- Prefer the user's local notes, drafts, and provided references.
- Keep local searches scoped to the folders or files named by the user.
- For web-fetched content, cite the source URL and do not invent bibliographic details.

## Output

- For substantial writing, create or update a `.md` or `.tex` file rather than dumping a long article into chat.
- In chat, provide a short summary of the changes and the reasoning behind major structural choices.
