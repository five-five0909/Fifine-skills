## Usage

Icons are automatically matched when the prompt mentions a service name or alias.
The `build-request.py` script scans the prompt text and embeds matching PNGs into the Gemini request.

To force-include specific icons: `python3 scripts/build-request.py prompt.txt request.json --icons "Cloud Storage" "BigQuery"`
